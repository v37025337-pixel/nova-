# Digital Mind Development V1

## Primary objective

Develop a digital mind by building mechanisms that generate, test, revise,
plan, transfer, and improve — rather than hard-coding final answers.

A capability is not considered real until it passes held-out or independent
tests. REJECT and UNRESOLVED are valid outcomes.

## Sequence

1. **DM01 — Contextual Belief Arbitration → M0013**
   - Resolve apparent belief conflicts by scope/context.
   - Preserve both provenance chains.
   - Never force a winner without discriminating evidence.
   - PASS requires at least 4 held-out conflict cases and support for:
     scope split, unresolved conflict, and discriminating-evidence goal birth.

2. **DM02 — Causal Self-Model → M0014**
   - Model the kernel's own mechanisms, resources, limits, goals and failure modes.
   - Must predict consequences of its own actions on held-out runtime changes.

3. **DM03 — Goal and Interest Architecture → M0015**
   - Represent competing goals, curiosity, utility, cost, risk, deadlines and budgets.
   - Select what to pursue and explain why.

4. **DM04 — Multi-Step Planning and Replanning → M0016**
   - Build dependency-aware plans.
   - Execute permitted steps.
   - Detect failure and replan instead of repeating blindly.

5. **DM05 — Counterfactual World Model → M0017**
   - Predict before acting.
   - Compare prediction with observation.
   - Update causal models from prediction error.

6. **DM06 — Metacognition and Calibration → M0018**
   - Estimate confidence.
   - Recognize insufficient evidence.
   - Ask for missing evidence.
   - Measure calibration against actual outcomes.

7. **DM07 — Generic Mechanism Genesis → M0019**
   - Deficit → candidate mechanism → evaluator → shadow → reject/redesign/admit.
   - No mechanism admission without an independent evaluator.

8. **DM08 — Cross-Domain Cognitive Transfer → M0020**
   - Reuse learned mechanisms in unseen domains without hand-coded adapters.

9. **DM09 — Persistent Self-History → M0021**
   - Append-only causal record of beliefs, revisions, mechanisms and goal changes.

10. **DM10 — Autonomous Cognitive Evolution → M0022**
    - Observe → deficit → goal → research → hypothesis → falsify → revise →
      mechanism → shadow → regression → admit → next deficit.
    - Requires at least 20 autonomous cycles, 5 self-generated deficits and
      preserved regression behavior.

## Operating rules

- Do not encode answers; build mechanisms that arrive at answers.
- Preserve provenance and old versions.
- No production self-modification without shadow tests and regression checks.
- Internet/browser actions remain bounded by admitted mechanisms.
- Prefer causal explanations over correlation-only descriptions.
- NOT_REJECT is not proof of truth.
- Never silently claim background activity; every autonomous step must appear in
  the runtime cycle journal.
