# Unified NOVA experiment

`migration.json` records full semantic verification of the Nova G22 and Next G2
parent histories. `nova_core/cognition/bootstrap.json.gz` preserves their complete
projected states, including the pending graph candidate and prior failed attempts.

`live/protocol.json` pins the new shared runtime before the Tenacity and Tornado
downloads. The frozen inherited candidate passed 24/24 fresh views, compared with
18/24 for the best baseline and 8/24 after removal of its acquired dependency.
It was admitted only after regression of both capability families.

The same controller then planned and executed nine actions across the inherited
Nova naming skill, source capture, UCR, three acquired graph programs, proof-based
logic and report assembly. The report goal was supplied by the operator; the plan
was selected by the kernel. The evolution goal and candidate were inherited from
the previous native cycle, without changing their code.

`live/journal.json.gz` is the trained unified checkpoint. `live/verification.json`
records full offline replay, cold restart, 192/192 legacy answers, 72/72 graph
checks and rollback with retained exposure history. Use `scripts/verify_unified.py`
to recompute the verification on Python 3.12 / Linux x86-64.

The supplied cognitive algorithms and finite languages are engineering mechanisms.
No consciousness or unrestricted intelligence claim is made. Captured upstream
sources retain their upstream licenses; texts are under `live/upstream-licenses/`.
